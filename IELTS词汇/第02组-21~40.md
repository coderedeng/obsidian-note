# 雅思高频词汇 第2组 (21-40)

> 生成日期: 2026-05-25
> 来源: 雅思官方高频词汇库

---

## 21. Campaign
- **音标**: /kæmˈpeɪn/
- **词性**: n. / v.
- **释义**: 运动，战役；参选活动
- **例句**: The company launched a campaign to raise awareness.
- **搭配**: advertising campaign / political campaign

## 22. Categorize
- **音标**: /ˈkætəɡaɪz/
- **词性**: v.
- **释义**: 分类，归类
- **例句**: Researchers categorized the data into several groups.
- **搭配**: categorize into / be categorized as

## 23. Chronic
- **音标**: /ˈkrɒnɪk/
- **词性**: adj.
- **释义**: 慢性的；长期的
- **例句**: She suffers from chronic back pain.
- **搭配**: chronic disease / chronic problem

## 24. Circumstance
- **音标**: /ˈsɜːkəmstəns/
- **词性**: n.
- **释义**: 环境，情况；境遇
- **例句**: Under no circumstances should you give up.
- **搭配**: under the circumstances / due to circumstances

## 25. Climate
- **音标**: /ˈklaɪmət/
- **词性**: n.
- **释义**: 气候；风气，氛围
- **例句**: Climate change is a global concern.
- **搭配**: climate change / public climate

## 26. Collaborate
- **音标**: /kəˈlæbəreɪt/
- **词性**: v.
- **释义**: 合作，协作
- **例句**: Scientists from different countries collaborated on the project.
- **搭配**: collaborate with / collaborate on

## 27. Collapse
- **音标**: /kəˈlæps/
- **词性**: v. / n.
- **释义**: 倒塌；崩溃
- **例句**: The economy collapsed in the 1930s.
- **搭配**: collapse under / economic collapse

## 28. Command
- **音标**: /kəˈmɑːnd/
- **词性**: n. / v.
- **释义**: 命令，指挥；掌握
- **例句**: She has a good command of three languages.
- **搭配**: under command / take command

## 29. Commit
- **音标**: /kəˈmɪt/
- **词性**: v.
- **释义**: 犯（罪）；承诺，投入
- **例句**: The government committed to reducing emissions.
- **搭配**: commit to / commit oneself to

## 30. Comparable
- **音标**: /ˈkɒmpərəbl/
- **词性**: adj.
- **释义**: 可比较的；相似的
- **例句**: The results are comparable with last year's figures.
- **搭配**: comparable to / of comparable quality

## 31. Compete
- **音标**: /kəmˈpiːt/
- **词性**: v.
- **释义**: 竞争，比赛
- **例句**: Companies must compete in a global market.
- **搭配**: compete with / compete for

## 32. Comprehensive
- **音标**: /ˌkɒmprɪˈhensɪv/
- **词性**: adj.
- **释义**: 全面的，综合的
- **例句**: A comprehensive review of the literature was conducted.
- **搭配**: comprehensive report / comprehensive study

## 33. Compel
- **音标**: /kəmˈpel/
- **词性**: v.
- **释义**: 强迫，迫使
- **例句**: The evidence compelled us to change our view.
- **搭配**: compel sb to do / be compelled to

## 34. Complement
- **音标**: /ˈkɒmplɪment/
- **词性**: v. / n.
- **释义**: 补充，补足；赞美
- **例句**: The wine complements the food perfectly.
- **搭配**: complement each other / full complement

## 35. Component
- **音标**: /kəmˈpəʊnənt/
- **词性**: n.
- **释义**: 组成部分，成分
- **例句**: Each component plays a vital role in the system.
- **搭配**: key component / major component

## 36. Comprise
- **音标**: /kəmˈpraɪz/
- **词性**: v.
- **释义**: 包含，由……组成
- **例句**: The committee comprises representatives from all departments.
- **搭配**: comprise all / be comprised of

## 37. Conceive
- **音标**: /kənˈsiːv/
- **词性**: v.
- **释义**: 构想，构思；怀孕
- **例句**: He conceived the idea while traveling in Asia.
- **搭配**: conceive of / be conceived as

## 38. Confine
- **音标**: /kənˈfaɪn/
- **词性**: v.
- **释义**: 限制，禁闭
- **例句**: Patients were confined to their rooms.
- **搭配**: confine to / within the confines of

## 39. Confirm
- **音标**: /kənˈfɜːm/
- **词性**: v.
- **释义**: 确认，证实
- **例句**: Can you confirm your attendance?
- **搭配**: confirm that / confirm with

## 40. Congestion
- **音标**: /kənˈdʒestʃən/
- **词性**: n.
- **释义**: 拥挤；堵塞
- **例句**: Traffic congestion is a major problem in big cities.
- **搭配**: traffic congestion / flight congestion
